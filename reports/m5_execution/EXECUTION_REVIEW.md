# M5 real-source accounting validation

Purpose is `ENGINEERING_ACCOUNTING_VALIDATION`, `data_origin=REAL`, `synthetic=false`. The registered account is an engineering scenario, not a historical broker account or an Alpha experiment. Initial cash is CNY10m solely to exercise two 100-share Moutai lots within the unchanged 5% cap; deployment capital is unchanged. The normal stock-dividend strategy entry remains blocked. R2/R3 real strategy experiments remain zero and no 2025+ prices were accessed.

`registration.json` freezes three windows and integer targets before new collection. `registration_addendum.json` records the synthetic affordable-gap counterexample and the fixed-quantity semantic repair before real prices were fetched. Fixed share instructions reserve against the verified daily upper price bound at opening; cash, risk, lot, T+1, historical capacity and adverse-limit gates still apply. Weight-based portfolio instructions retain their existing reservation semantics. The bound must come from prior raw close and known ex-date cash adjustment plus the dated historical rule; a calculated bound is not mislabeled as a vendor-supplied limit. Seasoned-listing checks use a documented count lower bound from archived sessions, not an invented exact IPO ordinal.

The remaining gaps are applicable 2023 amendments, explicit stock settlement-day evidence, the base stamp-duty rule and 2015/2022 CSDC Shanghai transfer-fee originals. The 2018 text does not by itself prove an unbroken rule chain into 2023. Known 2015 individual-dividend tax/FIFO and 2023 stamp-duty-halving originals do not fill these gaps. Those linked future documents were not fetched. The old 2022 fee PDF again returned 404. No source requirement was relaxed. Candidate fee rates have `verified=false`, with no invented publication clock.

The actual real manifest enters `run_backtest(..., engineering_replay=True)`. With the rule gaps retained, all three actual ledgers reject trading, preserve CNY10m cash and have zero fills. The 110 rows reconcile to independently expected gate-blocked balances. This demonstrates the source gate; it does **not** validate the three events with live positions. Each case is labeled BLOCKED, not PASS.

Separate `*_conditional_expected.csv` and `*_conditional_predicted_fills.json` are input-only Decimal calculations using explicitly unverified candidate rules. ## Cash clocks and limitations

The registered scenario recognizes entitlement after record-date trading, receivable on ex-date and usable cash one verified exchange session after the published pay date. The modeled debit is the scheduled sale date plus one verified session; it remains absent if no payable arises. Published payment date, model credit, tax accrued/payable, modeled debit/paid and potential terminal tax are distinct. `actual_pay_date` and `actual_debit_date` remain null and broker settlement is UNVERIFIED. No broker ledger or personal historical-account access is required to test this model, and none was requested.

Reserve restricts available/withdrawable cash; it is not a second NAV expense. Main NAV deducts accrued payable once. Hypothetical liquidation NAV additionally deducts potential holding-period tax. Special dividend identity is retained and is never supplied twice to cover the provider omission. Unknown delisting/recovery dates are not invented: this registered real case uses known Kangmei ST instead. Existing unknown-right synthetic tests continue to retain shares with zero uncertain carrying value and no invented cash. Share bonus/conversion, rights, mergers and stock liquidation remain explicit rejects.

Historical daily vendor bars establish retrospective observations, not historical first receipt. Their 21:00 clock is an explicitly modeled daily-close availability clock; `vendor_first_seen_at` is null. The engine trusts validated normalized records; archive hashes prove byte identity, not that any arbitrary source declaration is authentic. The real-case adapter additionally checks normalized price/basic fields against hash-pinned original SDK records, and verifies event/rule document hashes without silently replacing registered hashes. Initial normal state is anchored to the first window observation and the public ST notice supplies the transition clock. Engineered permission and single-issuer industry bucket are assumptions, not historical broker permission or a PIT industry claim. Daily bars cannot establish opening queue execution. Fixed-case, source-conditional accounting is not executable-strategy admission. Prefix/revision tests use explicitly invented contract inputs and do not turn conditional real tables into accepted evidence.

## Validation

Author regression: 199 accounting-related tests passed in 8.16 seconds; this includes the M4 and new M5 contract tests. Independent reviewer round 1 ran 37 tests: four failures exposed a copied-order flag omission, contradictory origin declarations, source-hash repinning and an obscured limit-mismatch reason. One corrective round repaired all four. Independent round 2 passed all 42 tests in 2.63 seconds, including separately authored raw-input Decimal oracles for all 110 conditional daily rows, terminal unknown rights and five failed sale attempts. The reviewer test files were not edited by this author. These are independent automated/agent checks, not human admission signoff. No CAGR, Sharpe, Alpha or annualized-performance metrics are produced. No M4 reports were rewritten.

Final collection is bounded and closed: 25 execution HTTP-budget slots (23 direct HTTP attempts plus two root metadata-tool calls), seven BaoStock wire attempts and 796,554 recorded response bytes. The two metadata slots measure complete visible tool-output bytes, not unobserved service-internal HTTP or transport bytes; the shared recovery audit preserves the original failure and correction. Remaining five HTTP slots were not exhausted without a defensible original-document candidate. Source additions changed evidence/report artifacts only after independent round 2.
