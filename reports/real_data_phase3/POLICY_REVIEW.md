# Independent policy review before historical returns

Review date: 2026-11-15 Asia/Shanghai. This review interprets the frozen Phase 1 documents; it does not approve a Dataset or certify strategy results. No strategy returns were used to select these policies. Development remains through 2021, selection validation 2022–2024, and the 2025–2026 final holdout remains locked.

The contract permits real historical data in a hypothetical model. It does not require personal broker receipts or a vendor's original timestamp for every permitted availability mode. Conversely, a model assumption must not become a fabricated observation, an undocumented strategy change, or a blanket PASS.

| Question | Required proof | Requirement that would be unnecessarily strong |
|---|---|---|
| Historical prices | Identified raw unadjusted observations, calendar/status checks, reproducible source lineage, explicit availability reconstruction and revision limitations | Original vendor first-seen timestamps when the declared mode is `historical_public_asof` |
| Hypothetical cash dividend | Official entitlement, amount and payment notice; applicable settlement mechanism; documented exceptions; explicit simulated credit policy | A receipt from a personal account that did not historically hold the hypothetical position |
| Research use | Evidence supporting the actual local private research use; enforce the resulting restrictions | Rights to commercial redistribution, hosting or execution services when none is undertaken |
| Historical universe | Complete relevant historical candidate inventory, exact underlying identity and deterministic competition | Full return reconstruction for a candidate demonstrably unused by any strategy/benchmark dependency |
| Economic and execution confirmation | Historical rule coverage, honest fill limitations and the frozen statistical/economic gates | Treating a daily research curve as verified intraday execution or realized investment performance |

The first two distinctions follow DATA_CONTRACT §§2–3 and §5.3. The contract explicitly permits public-information reconstruction and leaves `vendor_first_seen_at` nullable. Its rule on cash refers to economic availability, not mandatory evidence from a nonexistent hypothetical account. RISK_POLICY and EXPERIMENT_PLAN still constrain the conclusions drawn from modeled results.

**Price formation and availability.** Exchange rules establish public trading quotations and the session clock. Shanghai's 2014 rules publish real-time securities and index quotations; the 2020 and 2023 rules retain public quotation provisions. These support reconstructing a completed domestic exchange session's observable raw market prices for an evening decision, with a declared processing buffer. They do not establish when BaoStock received a particular historical bar. Store market-event time, assumed research availability, actual ingestion and unknown vendor first-seen separately. Do not label an inferred 21:00 research cutoff an observed publication timestamp. [SSE 2014 rules](https://www.sse.com.cn/aboutus/mediacenter/hotandd/c/c_20150912_3988782.shtml), [SSE 2020 rules](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules/repeal/rules/c/10118619/files/b39b8db2daa74eac916d8b3d6907af51.docx), [SSE 2023 rules](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/repeal/rules/c/10824490/files/dcbe58edb194451d93f19b1f7dd8fb4c.docx).

BaoStock's present help page describes daily data publication around 17:30 and local analysis. That is corroboration of the current service, not a historical service-level guarantee. Its revision log explicitly records historical corrections, including corrections to 2020-04-20 daily OHLC made on 2020-05-04. Current historical responses therefore cannot be called original vintages. Cross-check material observations, identify relevant documented corrections, and quarantine unresolved conflicts. A public reconstruction may use verified exchange facts while disclosing that the vendor's original erroneous return is unavailable; it cannot claim to reproduce that vendor's historical feed. [BaoStock help](https://baostock.com/helpdocs/api/markdown/home.md), [revision log](https://baostock.com/helpdocs/api/markdown/modifyRecord.md).

CSI's captured 2023 methodology states that closing indices are published each index trading day and distinguishes price, gross total-return and net total-return indices. It does **not** supply an exact 21:00 publication guarantee or establish a 2015–2022 timestamp history. The already captured H00300 values establish data access, not an original-vintage or clock proof. If admitting those values for same-evening signals, disclose and separately justify a reconstructed daily-availability policy; do not silently convert a document date into an observed precise timestamp. If only a date-only release is supportable, apply the frozen conservative availability rule, and record its effect on which prior observations enter the unchanged signal formula. Uncertain availability is a field-specific issue, not a reason to pretend H00300 does not exist. [CSI 2023 calculation and maintenance methodology](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/notice/20230908165124-%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E8%AF%81%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E8%82%A1%E7%A5%A8%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E4%B8%8E%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4%E7%BB%86%E5%88%99%E3%80%8B.pdf).

**Ordinary dividend policy.** ChinaClear's Shanghai ETF guide, 2022 version, pp.17–18, requires funding before payment and disclosure if payment cannot be made on time. On receipt of funds, ChinaClear transfers them to the designated securities firm after the preceding trading day's close; investors may collect on the payment date. The 2021 version provides the same ordinary-cash mechanism. The modeled baseline can therefore credit an ordinary Shanghai ETF dividend on its official payable date where the historical event notice and applicable rule support that mechanism, the hypothetical account has designated trading, and no postponement overrides it. An intraday credit time remains an assumption: do not assert that an actual broker credited this account before a particular minute. [ChinaClear 2022 guide](https://www.chinaclear.cn/zdjs/editor_file/20220616135839106.pdf), [2021 guide](https://www.chinaclear.cn/zdjs/editor_file/20210709112758380.pdf).

Use distinct fields, for example `cash_credit_basis=rule_verified_simulated`, `actual_receipt_at=null`, a rule/source reference, announced payable date, modeled credit time and explicit exceptions. Never set `actual_account_verified=true` merely because this policy is accepted. A boolean originally meaning observed receipt must be replaced or narrowed, not relabeled behind the scenes. Record entitlement from eligible record-date shares, receivable recognition separately, and cash only once. Raw prices govern the ledger; total-return features must not add a second cash payment.

This evidence is Shanghai-specific and versioned. Earlier periods and Shenzhen events need contemporary issuer payment wording or applicable historical settlement evidence; a 2022 Shanghai guide alone does not prove every ETF's 2015 settlement. Fixed +1 and +3 trading-session cash-delay scenarios are useful sensitivity checks declared before results, not replacements chosen after seeing profits. If the notice promises only a transfer to an intermediary or expressly makes actual arrival dependent on it, use a justified conservative model or preserve a blocked cash state. The special 512320 liquidation notice must not inherit the ordinary-dividend rule; retain that audit case even though its MSCI underlying falls outside this R1B interpretation.

**Share changes and later-finalized terms.** A previously announced conversion formula, record-date entitlement, exact later-finalized ratio, economic effective time and usable/tradable share time are separate facts. Do not backfill a subsequently announced exact ratio into an earlier decision. A formula established before effectiveness can create a contingent entitlement; it does not imply its inputs or finalized rounded share count were known. Preserve issuer-specific floor/ceiling rules, end-of-day versus beginning-of-day application, canceled plans and replacements. Preserve suspension and resumption intervals. A suspension may prevent an erroneous trade, but does not by itself cure look-ahead in earlier valuation, portfolio weights, liquidity selection or risk calculations. Any such dependency needs an explicit contingent state or an unavailable signal. This review does not certify the newly collected 510500/512100 ratios; the issuer documents and engine replay remain separate gates.

**Historical trading rules.** The archived primary texts support the following base constraints for ordinary domestic equity ETF secondary-market trading; product-specific exceptions take priority.

| Period/evidence | Supported implementation obligation |
|---|---|
| SSE 2014/2020/2023 general rules | Buy quantities in 100-unit lots, fund tick RMB0.001, ordinary 10% limits, no resale before settlement unless an explicit same-day exception applies |
| SZSE 2013 and 2016 general rules; 2022 fund rules; 2023 general rules | Version the corresponding fund lot/tick/price-limit and settlement rules rather than assigning stock rules to every ETF |

These sources do not constitute a complete row-by-row historical rule audit. Effective and repeal dates, ex-right reference prices, rounding, suspended days, listing exceptions and special market-wide halts must still be mapped. In particular, a naïve prior-close-times-limit calculation is insufficient around distributions/conversions. A frozen current-account cost scenario may be reported as counterfactual costs under DATA_CONTRACT §6; it is not historical commission evidence. Missing minute/queue observations continue to limit daily output to research screening, with the prescribed participation, unfilled-order and cost stresses. Do not call daily fills verified 09:35–10:00 executions.

**Deterministic R1B scope.** The frozen wording is broader than five hardcoded index codes. The minimal consistent category rule retains the three explicitly named mainland size indices (CSI300/500/1000), and broad, non-sector, non-theme, non-style representative indices of ChiNext, STAR, or their combination that actually existed and had tradable products then. This includes plausible ChiNext50, STAR100 and STAR+ChiNext50 families for documentary classification; names alone are insufficient. The explicit BSE clause requires actual product existence and the same conditions, while the user's lack of BSE permission remains independently binding. Theme/industry/style/ESG/dividend/volatility, overseas, leveraged/inverse and multiasset products are not interchangeable broad-market slots. Active/enhanced products require a separately justified classification rather than silently treating them as passive replicas.

This is a prospective interpretation of ambiguous category wording, not a claim that the frozen text uniquely enumerated every eligible code. Freeze it in the decision log before returns, keep doubtful candidates with documented disposition, and never choose scope according to return or collection convenience. Each exact tracked index has its own competition; do not merge related index families. Include all historically competing ETFs, including subsequently closed products, before applying the prior-60-day turnover rule and tie breakers. Quarterly representation and per-signal 252-session age/20-day liquidity/account-status eligibility are distinct steps.

**Dependency-aware collection.** It is valid to compute the complete quarterly representative union using historical candidate inventory, exact-index mappings, raw turnover and applicable selection status, before calculating returns. Nonselected candidates may then retain `usage_scope=REPRESENTATIVE_SELECTION_ONLY` and null TR. The audit must independently recompute every quarter from the complete candidate inputs and prove that TR availability, returns and future survival never enter selection. Full event/TR coverage is required for every selected representative, every baseline/benchmark exposure and every held instrument, including all necessary preselection lookback and postreplacement holding periods. If a selected instrument lacks required events, block the dependent research; never substitute the runner-up or omit that quarter. This optimization reduces unused work without deleting difficult candidates.

**Permitted source use.** BaoStock expressly offers its API for quantitative users and local data analysis; that supports the present free local research scope. CSI's official website legal statement permits noncommercial browsing/download subject to law and its terms, while restricting resale/product promotion and redistribution. Keep its raw snapshots private and ignored by Git, provide hashes/provenance and derived research summaries, and do not assert commercial data-distribution or execution-service rights. This is a scoped reading for the work undertaken, not a blanket license opinion. No logins, payments, contacts or circumvention were used.
