## Source-clock decision

**Public raw domestic exchange bars can use a documented evening reconstruction.** DATA_CONTRACT §2 explicitly permits `historical_public_asof`. Requiring a historical vendor first-seen timestamp for that mode would contradict the contract. Exchange public quotation/session evidence can support observing the completed domestic session and processing it by the declared 21:00 decision clock. Store unknown `vendor_first_seen_at` and unobserved exact `published_at` as null. A new `reconstructed_market_close` precision/basis must be an explicit schema extension, restricted to the applicable market observations and supported by the policy source; it must not become a way to relabel arbitrary date-only announcements. An assumed 15:00 field represents the scheduled regular-session boundary unless an actual event time is independently known. Early market-wide closures and other exceptions must not be mislabeled as observed 15:00 trades.

Current downloaded corrections are not original vendor vintages. They can support a public-fact reconstruction with raw hashes, disclosed correction limitations and reconciliation of material known conflicts. They cannot establish how the vendor's historical erroneous feed would have behaved. This is a limit on the claim, not an automatic ban on daily research. The earlier policy review's captured exchange rules and BaoStock revision log supply the source evidence and its limitations.

**H00300 should use the frozen date-only rule for the primary research path.** The official 2023 methodology §6 states daily publication through official channels and daily closing-index publication. It does not establish a precise 21:00 historical release deadline. The present review independently verified its local text and the web-indexed 2006.11.13 CSI methodology: §4.6 defines the dividend-reinvestment total-return series, and §13.2, PDF page 23, states that CSI publishes it on its website daily after the close. That earlier source supports a historical public daily publication mechanism; it does not establish every original daily vintage or publication before 21:00. Initial retrieval attempts timed out/returned 403, but the same document's URL with a trailing slash exposed the indexed paragraph successfully. The older original PDF bytes were not locally archived by this reviewer. [CSI 2023 methodology](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/notice/20230908165124-%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E8%AF%81%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E8%82%A1%E7%A5%A8%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E4%B8%8E%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4%E7%BB%86%E5%88%99%E3%80%8B.pdf); [older CSI methodology mirror](https://www.glqh.com/u/cms/www/202201/241408186eig.pdf/).

The parent registered the conservative mapping in decision D039 before returns:

| Item | Fixed treatment |
|---|---|
| Signal anchor | Original last exchange session D of the month |
| Momentum and trend | Original formulas and windows ending at D; no replacement by a D−1 trend |
| Quarterly representative and monthly eligibility | Original historical selection and D qualification; no new asset admitted because of D+1 information |
| H00300 D availability | First exchange session after D, 21:00 |
| Actual decision | D+1, 21:00 for all E0/R0/E1/R1, including risk-scaled versions |
| Baseline execution | D+2 under the registered daily fill assumptions |
| Additional execution-delay stress | D+3, retaining the same D signal anchor |
| Order sizing and capacity | Latest public prices and historical ADV available at the actual decision; may update quantities, never the ranking, trend or D eligibility |
| Subsequent adverse status | Risk/execution layers may reject or reduce; cannot add an asset excluded at D |

Here D+1 denotes the next exchange session, not the next calendar date. This is a conservative implementation of the frozen availability rule and a matched information delay, not a new return-optimized signal. It must be registered in the decision log and manifests before any real run. `signal_anchor_date`, actual `decision_at`, `order_reference_date` and `execution_date` must remain separately inspectable. The schema audit and runner must enforce the registered release delay. Additional date-only-publication delay is a separate information-clock sensitivity when relevant; moving a fill alone cannot establish that a signal used only available data.

An optimistic D+1-execution curve would be conditional on an unverified CSI evening-availability assumption. A fixed later-execution stress cannot retroactively turn that assumption into an observed timestamp. The accepted primary path does not need that curve. End-of-sample date-only observations require an explicit boundary policy: use independently sourced calendar-only information if necessary, or retain a clearly unavailable terminal observation. Do not fabricate a trading day, open holdout prices, or remove the final account valuation to make an audit pass.

## Material implementation finding and verification

**Fixed during review:** `reconstruct_total_return` originally overwrote each raw bar's `available_at` with the later of public-market availability and corporate-action terms. Both quarterly selectors then used that delayed clock to filter raw turnover. A fund with the largest already-public turnover could lose the competition merely because its split ratio was finalized later. This violated the promised amount-only dependency rule.

The independent regression uses two invented competing funds, an end-of-quarter EOD split and a later ratio announcement. It initially failed because the lower-turnover fund won. The parent fixed the separation: raw `available_at` remains the market clock; `total_return_available_at` controls TR features. Both selectors now retain the correct winner. `bars_asof` preserves the known price/amount, masks the unavailable TR, and reveals that TR only after its own clock. The new strategy code independently masks TR and restricts every economic feature to D while releasing the decision on D+1.

Validation command: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest tests/test_admission_review.py tests/test_dependencies.py tests/test_share_events.py tests/test_settlement.py tests/test_real_builder.py tests/test_strategy.py -q` — **59 passed**. This covers the independent timing regression plus synthetic checks for account-level share rounding, BOD/EOD conversions, suspended old-unit quotes, pending terms, hypothetical ordinary cash and fixed +1/+3 cash delays. The parent's new release-delay tests establish that date-only H00300 delays the original anchored formula and that D+1 price/benchmark changes cannot alter D's weights, while D+1 quotes can size orders. It is not a real-data strategy run or blanket engine certification.
