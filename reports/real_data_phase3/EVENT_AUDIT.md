Official event and historical metadata audit, 2015–2024

This is an evidence collection and reconciliation milestone. It contains no strategy performance, simulated profitability claim, live trading, paid access, or original-vintage database claim. All downloads used anonymous public exchange endpoints. Raw responses, PDFs, request parameters, errors, and SHA-256 hashes remain in ignored `data/raw_r1_actions/` directories.

The final event manifest is `reports/real_data_phase3/event_manifest.json`, SHA-256 `41eddae9a56ee8b0f3b797659788ae6f9c6b37607b9d15b29a62c0f0a0119e33`. It holds 101 complete per-instrument query batches, 231 downloaded official PDFs, 115 annual reports, 53 source event revisions, and 29 reconciled actions: 18 dividends and 11 executed share changes. All document downloads and text extraction succeeded. Eighteen annual reports published in 2025 describe 2024 and serve only retrospective completeness checks; they are not signal inputs or 2025 market observations.

Source queries use the official [SSE announcement endpoint](https://query.sse.com.cn/commonQuery.do) with `COMMON_PL_JJXX_JJGG_NEW_L`, and the [SZSE announcement endpoint](https://www.szse.cn/api/disc/announcement/annList) with `fundinfoNotice_disc`. Parameters, pagination totals and each raw response hash are in the archived query manifests. SZSE title searches require `searchKey`; an initial `keyword` probe was ignored by the service and was not accepted as a filtered query. The collector now rejects ignored title/date filters, repeated pages, changing totals, mis-keyed instruments, incomplete pagination, and hash corruption. Requests have bounded retries, byte limits and parent-process wall-clock deadlines. One 2022 annual report exceeded the initial 30 MB limit and was successfully retrieved with an explicit 100 MB bound.

Date-only notices become available at 21:00 Asia/Shanghai on the strictly following trading session. Economic dates remain source facts. Publication date is taken from the exchange record, not guessed from the PDF URL directory. Numeric terms published after an event never become available retroactively.

No record becomes engine-ready merely because it parsed. Consumers must honor the documented phase, knowledge time, cash convention and entitlement units. For Shenzhen EOD changes, `effective_date` and `registration_date` identify the actual EOD share change, while `ex_date` preserves the explicitly published market ex-date. Treating both fields as the same day would apply old-unit quotes to new-unit holdings. Raw unadjusted prices supplied by the data collector corroborate the quote transitions; notices remain the source of legal event facts.

The [159922 combined notice](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2024-11-15/74c9f510-906c-4a44-84da-69b812348e99.PDF), published November 16, specifies a dividend of CNY 0.1292 per **post-split unit**, record date November 29, ex-date December 2 and scheduled cash date December 3. The manifest explicitly records `POST_SPLIT_REGISTERED_UNITS`. Page 3 says cash leaves the custodian account on December 3; investor arrival follows CSDC and sales-institution rules. It does not verify personal receipt or guarantee broker availability that day. Its `cash_schedule_meaning` is therefore `CUSTODIAN_TRANSFER_DATE`; the importer needs a Shenzhen rule or an explicitly approved simulated cash-lag convention.

The other seventeen dividend notices include event-specific issuer language supporting receipt on the declared cash-dividend date for ordinary holders with a designated broker. This includes contemporary evidence before the 2021 settlement guide: the [510300 January 2015 notice](https://www.sse.com.cn/disclosure/fund/announcement/c/2015-01-13/510300_20150114_1.pdf), PDF page 3, describes the CSDC-to-designated-broker transfer and investor receipt on the announced date. The 2016 notice repeats this mechanism. The manifest retains page references and the relevant statements for every such event. These are grounds for a rule-based historical simulation; `actual_pay_date` remains null. See `POLICY_REVIEW.md` and `policy_evidence.json` for separately archived settlement rules. Shanghai guide scope is not silently extended to Shenzhen or earlier periods.

Annual reconciliation explicitly checks cash distributions against per-unit annual totals or unequivocal no-distribution statements. No-event share evidence comes from the report's share-change row, including wrapped labels and explicit dashes; a negative number is never interpreted as a dash. Missing text, missing annuals or an empty announcement search never becomes zero. Of 118 instrument-year coverage rows, 115 are COMPLETE. All years needed by the fifteen potentially age-eligible representatives are complete.

Three coverage rows remain incomplete. 159573 has no collected 2023 annual share-change statement; its 2024 annual explicitly says no dividends since inception but proves no share change only for 2024. Official listing was December 25, 2023, and the verified calendar contains only 247 listed sessions through December 31, 2024. It cannot satisfy the frozen 252-session age condition in the research window. This supports a separately checked feature-dependency waiver while preserving it in quarterly representative competition and leaving unsupported 2023 total-return values null. It does not establish that no event occurred. The other two incomplete rows are 512320 in 2023 and 2024, outside the R1B selected union.

The 512320 lifecycle chain remains preserved in the earlier `reports/real_data/official_events.json` and `OFFICIAL_EVENT_AUDIT.md`, plus new original annual and lifecycle captures: listing August 7, 2020; one-day halt January 24, 2024; permanent halt from February 23; liquidation beginning February 24; March 28 distribution notice, March 29 record date, scheduled April 8 payment of CNY 0.77524 per original unit; and April 16 delisting. Financial no-event completeness for all of 2023–2024 and personal cash receipt are not asserted. Source-event dates must not be replaced with an invented accounting recognition date.

Original documents correct historical membership, not just dates. [159908's December 4, 2018 conversion notice](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk01/finalpage/2018-12-04/b95e617d-4da6-4a23-8c99-e2344ab45ba6.PDF) explicitly changes the benchmark from the fundamental-200 fund to the ChiNext index on December 10. The master has an original OUTSIDE row and a later CHINEXT/R1B row available December 5 at 21:00. The independent amount-only selector then drops 159908 from the representative dependency union. A current benchmark was not backdated to its 2011 listing. The original 510500 listing PDF contains a 2012 date typo; the exchange's same-day final version establishes March 15, 2013. Both originals are retained. The scanned 159925 notice was visually inspected: PDF page 3 establishes April 11, 2013, and its legal fund name.

The master also retains dated official delisting revisions for six historical competitors: 510520 on October 31, 2019; 515930 on July 31, 2023; 159846 on January 5, 2022; 159927 on February 14, 2018; 159955 on November 30, 2022; and 159999 on March 20, 2023. Legal delisting is separate from suspension, liquidation and the last executed market trade. No last-trade date is inferred from a missing bar or final snapshot. Their cash/liquidation ledgers are not treated as complete; the independent representative-dependency check must establish that they were never held before waiving those ledger details.
